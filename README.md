# Simulation of Local Energy Communities

Agent-based AnyLogic simulation of household PV, community wind generation and peer-to-peer electricity trading.

**Tools:** AnyLogic, Agent-based simulation, Excel.

## Project focus

- Model households in a Danish energy community
- Compare PV, community wind and peer-to-peer trading scenarios
- Evaluate electricity costs, emissions and sensitivity
